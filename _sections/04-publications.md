  <h3 class="pub-title">
    Hardware-Efficient Acceleration of Spiking Neural Networks on Low-Power Embedded FPGA Platforms
  </h3>

  <div class="pub-authors">
    <span class="author-self">Pierre-Louis Sixdenier</span>, 
    <span>[Co-Author Placeholder]</span>, 
    <span>[Advisor Placeholder]</span>
  </div>

  <div class="pub-abstract">
    <strong>Abstract:</strong> Neuromorphic Spiking Neural Networks (SNNs) offer immense power advantages for edge devices. We present a pipelined, event-driven hardware accelerator synthesized on low-power FPGA architectures, minimizing memory footprint and reducing dynamic switching energy during temporal spike propagation.
  </div>
