  <h3 class="pub-title">
    Contrastive Representation Learning with Adaptive Geometry for Low-Resource Multimodal Tasks
  </h3>

  <div class="pub-authors">
    <span class="author-self">Pierre-Louis Sixdenier</span>, 
    <span>Jane Doe</span>, 
    <span>Alex Martin</span>
  </div>

  <div class="pub-abstract">
    <strong>Abstract:</strong> We propose a novel Riemannian contrastive objective that dynamically warps latent metric spaces according to local sample density. Our approach significantly reduces catastrophic representation collapse in low-resource multimodal alignment, boosting few-shot transfer performance by +4.8% on standard benchmarks while preserving linear probing efficiency.
  </div>

    Token-Sparse Vision Transformers: Adaptive Computation for Real-Time Dense Prediction
  </h3>

  <div class="pub-abstract">
    <strong>Abstract:</strong> Vision transformers suffer from quadratic complexity when applied to high-resolution feature maps. We introduce an entropy-guided routing policy that dynamically suppresses non-informative spatial tokens across intermediate layers without requiring fine-grained task annotations.

  <h3 class="pub-title">
    Provable Convergence Bounds for Gradient-Based Self-Distillation in Deep Ensembles
  </h3>

  <div class="pub-authors">
    <span class="author-self">Pierre-Louis Sixdenier</span>, 
    <span>Alex Martin</span>
  </div>

  <div class="pub-abstract">
    <strong>Abstract:</strong> While self-distillation has shown empirical improvements across vision and language benchmarks, theoretical guarantees remain scarce. In this work, we formalize self-distillation as an implicit regularizer under non-convex optimization and derive finite-time convergence bounds.
  </div>
